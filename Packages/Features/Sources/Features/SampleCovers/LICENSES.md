# Sample covers – origin and licenses

Generated by the build script `beispielcover.py` (the maintainer's private tooling, not part of this repository); do not edit by hand.

The covers of the preview data (`-attrappen`). Each image was scaled to at most 600 px (JPEG); the Pepper&Carrot artwork was also cropped to 2:3, and the audiobook covers were padded to a square with a dark border. Public-domain scans are marked as such on Wikimedia Commons; Pepper&Carrot is licensed CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en
